# Simulated results: nvme

Device `nvme` is a nominal model, not a measurement. 3 seeds, 20000 accesses per trace, cache = 5% of unique bytes, think time 100 us, prefetch depth 4, threshold 0.4. Values are medians across seeds. `speedup` is end-to-end time versus the LRU baseline (above 1.0 is faster). `coverage` is the share of LRU's demand misses avoided.
